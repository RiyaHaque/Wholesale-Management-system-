USER: SHOP MANAGER

FEATURE: LOW STOCK ALERT

Purpose:
The Low Stock Alert feature helps the Shop Manager identify products that need restocking.

Functions:

1. Set Minimum Stock Level
- A minimum stock threshold is defined for products.

2. Monitor Stock
- The system continuously checks product stock levels.

3. Generate Low Stock Alert
- When stock falls below the minimum level, the system should automatically generate an alert.

4. Display Alert
- The low-stock product should be clearly displayed in the inventory alerts section or dashboard.

5. Restock Product
- The Shop Manager can increase the stock quantity of the low-stock product.

6. Remove Alert
- When the stock quantity rises above the minimum threshold, the low-stock alert should be removed.

Example:
Initial Stock: 30 units
Minimum Threshold: 10 units
Updated Stock: 5 units
Result: Low Stock Alert
