USER: SHOP MANAGER / ADMIN

FEATURE: PRODUCT MANAGEMENT

Purpose:
The Product Management feature allows Admin and Shop Manager to manage shoe products in the system.

Functions:

1. Add Product
- Add a new shoe product to the system.
- Enter product name.
- Enter brand.
- Enter size.
- Enter category.
- Enter price.
- Enter stock quantity.
- Save the product.
- The product should appear in the product list.

2. Update Product
- Select an existing product.
- Edit product information.
- Update name, brand, size, category, price, or stock quantity.
- Save the changes.
- Updated information should be visible immediately.

3. View Product
- View available shoe products.
- View product details such as brand, size, category, price, and stock quantity.

4. Delete Product
- Remove an existing product from the product list.

Validation:
- The system should prevent invalid product information.
- The system should display an error when an invalid or non-existing product is selected.
