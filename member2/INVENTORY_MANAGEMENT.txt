USER: SHOP MANAGER

FEATURE: INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

Purpose:
The Inventory Management feature allows the Shop Manager to monitor and maintain accurate stock levels.

Functions:

1. View Inventory
- View current stock quantity of products.
- Monitor available shoe products.

2. Automatic Stock Update After Sale
- When a product is sold, the stock quantity should decrease automatically.

3. Automatic Stock Update After Purchase
- When new products are purchased, the stock quantity should increase automatically.

4. Automatic Stock Update After Return
- When a product is returned, the stock quantity should increase automatically.

5. Accurate Stock Tracking
- The system should always display the current stock quantity.

6. Inventory Validation
- The system should prevent invalid stock quantities.
- Invalid quantities should show an error message.

Example:
Initial Stock: 100 units
Sold Quantity: 50 units
Updated Stock: 50 units
